ENVIRONMENTAL SYSTEM

Building a Sustainable Food Future

The food industry impacts climate, land, water, and waste systems. Modern technology helps reduce damage and improve efficiency globally.

Key Environmental Impacts

Understanding how food systems affect our planet.

According to global environmental reports from organizations such as the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO), the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC), and the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP), food systems are one of the largest drivers of environmental change worldwide.

These impacts come from production, transportation, packaging, and waste management, affecting climate stability, ecosystems, and natural resources.

Climate Change

Greenhouse gases from agriculture and transport.

Deforestation

Forest clearing for farming reduces biodiversity.

Water Usage

Excess irrigation strains freshwater resources.

Waste

Packaging and food waste pollute ecosystems.
